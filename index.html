About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Industrial & Systems Engineering at the University of Florida with interests in advanced manufacturing, process optimization, and data-driven engineering.

My academic and professional experiences have allowed me to explore engineering from both research and industry perspectives. As an undergraduate researcher in the Interdisciplinary Manufacturing Engineering and Design (iMED) Lab, I investigate Directed Energy Deposition (DED) and its potential for advanced metal repair and remanufacturing.

Beyond research, I have worked on manufacturing engineering and continuous improvement projects involving production scheduling, process automation, root cause analysis, and inventory management. I am particularly interested in applying analytical methods to understand complex engineering systems and develop practical solutions.
